# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, friendly corner of the internet, and we'd like to keep it that way.

## Our standards

Everyone who takes part, whether in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project, is expected to:

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intentions, and give constructive feedback.
- Welcome newcomers and questions of every level.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and may block anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@JinHang02](https://github.com/JinHang02), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and fairly.

This code of conduct is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
